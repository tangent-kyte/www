FAQ

Questions, answered

Can you take over an existing codebase?

Yes. We start with a short code and architecture review, share what we find, then propose a plan to stabilize and extend it.

Do I need to know which services I need?

No. Tell us the problem or goal. During discovery we recommend the smallest set of services that gets you there.

Who owns the code and designs?

You do. All code, designs and documentation are yours, in your repositories and accounts, from day one.

How do you price projects?

Sprints are fixed-price. Dedicated teams and retainers are billed monthly. You get a written estimate after the discovery call, before any commitment.

How do we communicate during the project?

A shared channel for day-to-day questions, a live board for progress and a weekly demo where you see working software.
